# Adjust Your Workspace for Better Posture

## Short Health Tip  
Ensure your workspace is ergonomically set up to maintain proper posture, which can help prevent discomfort and strain during your work hours.

## Simple Explanation  
1. **Chair Height**: Adjust your chair so that your feet are flat on the floor, and your knees are at or just below hip level.  
2. **Screen Position**: Position your monitor at eye level to avoid bending your neck, keeping it about an arm's length away.  
3. **Desk Layout**: Place frequently used items within easy reach to avoid overreaching or twisting your body.  
4. **Keyboard and Mouse**: Keep your keyboard and mouse at the same height, allowing your elbows to remain close to your body.  
5. **Frequent Adjustments**: Remember to change your position regularly, as static postures can lead to fatigue and discomfort.

## Quick Tip  
Take a few minutes to adjust your chair and monitor height to create a more comfortable work environment that supports good posture.
