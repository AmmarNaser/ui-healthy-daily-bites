# Improve Your Posture While Sitting

## Short Health Tip
Maintaining good posture while sitting can reduce strain on your back and neck, leading to greater comfort throughout your day.

## Simple Explanation
1. **Point 1**: **Sit all the way back** in your chair to support your lower back and maintain the natural curve of your spine.
2. **Point 2**: **Adjust your chair height** so that your feet rest flat on the floor, allowing your knees to be at a 90-degree angle.
3. **Point 3**: **Keep your screen at eye level** to prevent straining your neck while looking up or down.
4. **Point 4**: **Take regular breaks** by standing or stretching every 30 minutes to relieve pressure on your muscles.
5. **Point 5**: **Use a lumbar support cushion** if your chair lacks proper support to help keep your spine aligned.

## Quick Tip
Set a timer on your phone to remind you to check your posture and stretch every half an hour to maintain comfort and reduce fatigue.
