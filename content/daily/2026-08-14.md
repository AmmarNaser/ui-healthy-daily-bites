# Protect Your Eyes from UV Rays

## Short Health Tip
Wearing sunglasses that block UV rays is crucial for maintaining healthy eyes, especially on bright sunny days.

## Simple Explanation
1. **Point 1**: **UV rays can harm your eyes**: Ultraviolet rays from the sun can lead to eye problems like cataracts and macular degeneration.
2. **Point 2**: **Sunglasses shield your eyes**: High-quality sunglasses provide a barrier that reduces the exposure of your eyes to harmful rays.
3. **Point 3**: **Look for 100% UV protection**: Ensure your sunglasses say they block 100% of UVA and UVB rays for maximum safety.
4. **Point 4**: **Wraparound styles offer more coverage**: Sunglasses that wrap around your face protect against rays that can enter from the sides.
5. **Point 5**: **Don’t forget them on cloudy days**: UV rays can penetrate clouds, so it's important to wear sunglasses even when it’s overcast.

## Quick Tip
Always choose sunglasses that block 100% of UV rays and wear them whenever you're outdoors to help preserve your eye health.
