# Snack Smart: Choose the Right Carbs

## Short Health Tip  
Opt for whole food snacks that contain healthy carbohydrates to help maintain stable blood sugar levels throughout the day.

## Simple Explanation  
1. **Whole grains are better**: Whole grain snacks, like oatmeal or whole-grain crackers, digest slowly, preventing spikes in blood sugar.  
2. **Add healthy fats**: Pairing carbs with healthy fats, such as nuts or avocados, can help slow down sugar absorption.  
3. **Include protein**: High-protein snacks, like yogurt or hummus with veggies, can keep you satisfied longer and help stabilize blood sugar.  
4. **Watch portions**: Even healthy snacks can impact blood sugar if eaten in large amounts, so it's important to keep portions in check.  
5. **Limit sugary snacks**: Refined sugars found in candy and pastries can cause rapid blood sugar spikes, so opt for healthier alternatives.

## Quick Tip  
Choose snacks like a handful of nuts or a piece of fruit with yogurt to keep your blood sugar steady and maintain energy levels.
