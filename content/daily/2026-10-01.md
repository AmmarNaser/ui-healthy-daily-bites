# Brighten Your Day with Eye-Healthy Foods

## Short Health Tip  
Incorporating eye-healthy foods into your diet can significantly improve your vision and overall eye health. Foods rich in vitamins C and E, omega-3 fatty acids, and antioxidants are particularly beneficial for your eyes.

## Simple Explanation  
1. **Leafy Greens**: Vegetables like spinach and kale are high in lutein and zeaxanthin, which help protect your eyes from harmful light.
2. **Colorful Fruits**: Oranges, berries, and carrots are rich in vitamin C and beta-carotene, both crucial for maintaining good vision.
3. **Fish**: Fatty fish such as salmon and tuna contain omega-3 fatty acids that can help reduce the risk of age-related macular degeneration.
4. **Nuts and Seeds**: Almonds, walnuts, and chia seeds are excellent sources of vitamin E, which helps protect the eyes from oxidative damage.
5. **Whole Grains**: Foods like brown rice and quinoa provide zinc, important for overall eye health and preventing night blindness.

## Quick Tip  
Add a handful of nuts to your afternoon snack or include leafy greens in your meals to boost your eye health effectively!
