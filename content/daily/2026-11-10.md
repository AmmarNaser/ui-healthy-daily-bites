# Incorporate Collagen for Stronger Bones and Joints

## Short Health Tip
Adding collagen to your diet can support bone and joint health by promoting flexibility and strength. It can be consumed through supplements or naturally by including bone broth in your meals.

## Simple Explanation
1. **Collagen's Role**: Collagen is a protein that helps maintain the structure and integrity of bones and joints.
2. **Supports Flexibility**: It improves flexibility in joints, potentially reducing the risk of injury.
3. **Aids in Bone Density**: Consuming collagen can help enhance bone density and overall strength.
4. **Promotes Joint Health**: It may alleviate joint pain and stiffness, making movement more comfortable.
5. **Natural Sources**: Foods like bone broth, chicken skin, and fish can naturally boost your collagen intake.

## Quick Tip
Try adding bone broth to soups or stews for an easy way to enrich your meals with collagen.
