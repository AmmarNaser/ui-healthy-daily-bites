# Give Your Eyes a Break with the 20-20-20 Rule

## Short Health Tip  
To reduce eye strain, remember the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, take a 20-second break to look at something 20 feet away.

## Simple Explanation  
1. **Point 1**: **Eye Strain Relief**: Staring at screens for long periods can lead to discomfort. The 20-20-20 rule helps give your eyes a needed rest.  
2. **Point 2**: **Focus Shifting**: By looking at something far away, you engage different eye muscles, promoting relaxation.  
3. **Point 3**: **Increased Comfort**: Regular breaks can decrease dry eyes and fatigue, making it easier to work or enjoy your screen time.  
4. **Point 4**: **Prevent Long-Term Damage**: Consistent eye strain may lead to longer-term issues; adopting this rule can help protect your vision.  
5. **Point 5**: **Better Productivity**: Taking brief breaks can enhance concentration and productivity when working or studying.  

## Quick Tip  
Set a timer on your phone or computer to remind you to take a 20-second break every 20 minutes to keep your eyes fresh and healthy.
