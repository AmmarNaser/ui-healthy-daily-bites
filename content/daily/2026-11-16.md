# Protect Your Eyes with a Balanced Diet

## Short Health Tip
Eating a variety of eye-healthy foods can help maintain your vision and protect your eyes from damage. Include colorful fruits and vegetables, nuts, and fish for optimal eye health.

## Simple Explanation
1. **Colorful Fruits and Vegetables**: Foods rich in vitamins A, C, and E, like carrots, spinach, and oranges, support eye health and help prevent conditions like cataracts.
2. **Omega-3 Fatty Acids**: Fish such as salmon and walnuts contain omega-3s, which can protect against dry eyes and macular degeneration.
3. **Zinc-Rich Foods**: Nuts, beans, and whole grains are high in zinc, which plays a crucial role in maintaining the health of the retina.
4. **Leafy Greens**: Spinach and kale provide lutein and zeaxanthin, antioxidants that help filter harmful light and protect the eyes from damage.
5. **Hydration**: Drinking plenty of water helps to keep your eyes moist and reduces the risk of dryness and irritation.

## Quick Tip
Try to include at least one eye-healthy food in every meal, like adding spinach to your salad or snacking on almonds, to boost your eye health daily.
