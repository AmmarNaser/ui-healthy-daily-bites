# Support Your Kidneys with Gentle Detox Methods

## Short Health Tip
Incorporating gentle detox methods can help support kidney function and overall health by promoting the elimination of toxins from the body.

## Simple Explanation
1. **Point 1**: **Increase Water Intake**: Drinking enough water helps flush out toxins and keeps the kidneys functioning properly.
2. **Point 2**: **Opt for Fresh Herbs**: Herbs like parsley and cilantro can aid in detoxification processes and support kidney health.
3. **Point 3**: **Limit Processed Foods**: Reducing intake of processed foods lowers the load on your kidneys, allowing them to focus on detoxing your body.
4. **Point 4**: **Include Quercetin-Rich Foods**: Foods like onions and apples contain quercetin, which can help protect kidney cells from damage.
5. **Point 5**: **Practice Regular Fasting**: Short-term fasting can give your kidneys a break and promote a natural detox process.

## Quick Tip
Try starting your day with a glass of warm water infused with lemon juice and parsley to kickstart your kidney detox routine.
