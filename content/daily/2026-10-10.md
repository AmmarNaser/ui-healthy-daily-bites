# Align Your Desk for Optimal Ergonomics

## Short Health Tip
Adjusting your desk and chair to fit your body can significantly improve your posture and comfort while working, reducing strain and enhancing productivity.

## Simple Explanation
1. **Desk Height**: Your desk should be at elbow height when seated, so your forearms are parallel to the ground.
2. **Chair Support**: Choose a chair that supports your lower back and allows your feet to rest flat on the floor or on a footrest.
3. **Monitor Position**: Position your computer monitor at eye level to prevent neck strain and encourage a more neutral head position.
4. **Keyboard and Mouse Placement**: Keep your keyboard and mouse close enough to eliminate reaching, allowing your arms to rest comfortably by your sides.
5. **Frequent Adjustments**: Regularly adjust your posture and take breaks to stand and stretch, promoting better circulation and reducing fatigue.

## Quick Tip
Invest in an adjustable chair and desk setup to ensure your workspace supports your body and encourages good posture throughout the day.
