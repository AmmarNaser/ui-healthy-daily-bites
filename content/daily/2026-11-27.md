# The Importance of Antioxidants for Immune Health

## Short Health Tip  
Incorporating antioxidant-rich foods into your diet can help strengthen your immune system and protect your body from harmful free radicals.

## Simple Explanation  
1. **Antioxidants Defined**: Antioxidants are compounds that help neutralize free radicals, which can damage cells and weaken the immune system.  
2. **Sources of Antioxidants**: Foods rich in antioxidants include berries, dark chocolate, nuts, and green leafy vegetables.  
3. **Protection Against Disease**: By fighting oxidative stress, antioxidants can reduce the risk of chronic diseases and enhance overall health.  
4. **Promoting Recovery**: Antioxidants also play a role in recovery by reducing inflammation after exercise or illness.  
5. **Balance is Key**: While antioxidants are beneficial, it's important to maintain a balanced diet that includes a variety of nutrients for optimal immune function.  

## Quick Tip  
Add a handful of mixed berries to your breakfast or snack for a delicious way to boost your antioxidant intake!
