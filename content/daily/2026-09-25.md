# The Role of Sleep in Immune Function

## Short Health Tip
Getting enough quality sleep is crucial for maintaining a strong immune system, as it helps your body repair and regenerate.

## Simple Explanation
1. **Quality Sleep Helps Repair**: During sleep, your body goes into repair mode, healing damaged tissues and strengthening the immune response.
2. **Regulation of Immune Cells**: Sleep influences the production and activity of immune cells, such as T cells, which are essential for fighting off infections.
3. **Hormonal Balance**: Sleep helps regulate hormones that support immune function, including cortisol, which can suppress immune activity if levels are too high.
4. **Prevention of Inflammation**: Adequate sleep reduces inflammation in the body, which can enhance overall immune responses.
5. **Enhanced Vaccine Response**: Good sleep can improve your body's response to vaccines, making them more effective in building immunity.

## Quick Tip
Aim for 7-9 hours of quality sleep each night to support your immune system and overall health.
