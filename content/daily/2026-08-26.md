# Set a Goal: Boost Your Exercise Motivation

## Short Health Tip
Setting achievable fitness goals can significantly boost your motivation to exercise regularly. Start with small, measurable targets that can help you stay focused and committed.

## Simple Explanation
1. **Point 1**: **Short-term goals**: Aim for small milestones, like exercising three times a week or walking 10,000 steps a day, to give yourself quick wins.
2. **Point 2**: **Long-term goals**: Set a larger objective, such as running a 5k or losing a specific amount of weight, to create a vision for your fitness journey.
3. **Point 3**: **Make it specific**: Clearly define what you want to achieve; for example, "I want to do 10 push-ups in a row" provides more direction than just saying "I want to get stronger."
4. **Point 4**: **Track your progress**: Keep a journal or use fitness apps to monitor your achievements, making it easier to see how far you've come.
5. **Point 5**: **Celebrate achievements**: Reward yourself for reaching milestones, whether that’s treating yourself to new workout gear or enjoying a favorite healthy meal.

## Quick Tip
Write down your fitness goals and keep them visible, like on your fridge or workout space, to remind you of what you're working toward each day.
