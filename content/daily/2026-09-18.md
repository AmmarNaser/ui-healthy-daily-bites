# Maintain a Heart-Healthy Weight

## Short Health Tip
Keeping a healthy weight is essential for heart health, as excess weight can strain your heart and increase the risk of heart disease.

## Simple Explanation
1. **Heart Strain**: Carrying extra weight puts additional pressure on your heart, making it work harder to pump blood.
2. **Blood Pressure**: Being overweight can lead to higher blood pressure, a major risk factor for heart disease.
3. **Cholesterol Levels**: Extra weight may raise bad cholesterol (LDL) and lower good cholesterol (HDL), impacting heart health.
4. **Diabetes Risk**: Maintaining a healthy weight can lower your risk of developing type 2 diabetes, which is linked to heart problems.
5. **Lifestyle Changes**: Small, consistent changes in diet and activity levels can help you achieve and maintain a healthy weight.

## Quick Tip
Aim to incorporate more fruits and vegetables into your meals while reducing processed foods to help manage your weight effectively.
