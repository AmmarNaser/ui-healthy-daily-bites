# Nourish Your Eyes with Omega-3 Fatty Acids

## Short Health Tip  
Incorporating omega-3 fatty acids into your diet can help support eye health and reduce the risk of age-related macular degeneration.

## Simple Explanation  
1. **Point 1**: Omega-3 fatty acids are essential fats that your body cannot produce on its own, making it important to include them in your diet.
2. **Point 2**: These healthy fats are found in fish like salmon, walnuts, flaxseeds, and chia seeds.
3. **Point 3**: Regular consumption of omega-3s can help lubricate the eyes and reduce dryness.
4. **Point 4**: They also support the structure of cell membranes in the retina, promoting better visual function.
5. **Point 5**: Including omega-3s may even help in preventing inflammation and protecting against eye diseases.

## Quick Tip  
Try adding a serving of fatty fish or a handful of walnuts to your meals a few times a week to boost your omega-3 intake!
