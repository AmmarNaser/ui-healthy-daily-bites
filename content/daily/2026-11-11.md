# Prioritize Your Calcium Intake for Stronger Bones

## Short Health Tip
Incorporating calcium-rich foods into your diet is essential for maintaining strong bones and preventing osteoporosis as you age.

## Simple Explanation
1. **Point 1**: **Calcium is vital**: It is a crucial mineral that helps build and maintain bone density.
2. **Point 2**: **Food sources**: Dairy products like milk, cheese, and yogurt, as well as leafy greens and fortified foods, are excellent sources of calcium.
3. **Point 3**: **Daily requirements**: Adults generally need about 1,000 mg of calcium daily, increasing to 1,200 mg for women over 50 and men over 70.
4. **Point 4**: **Combine with Vitamin D**: Vitamin D helps your body absorb calcium better, so consider getting enough sun exposure or taking supplements if necessary.
5. **Point 5**: **Monitor your intake**: Keep track of the calcium in your diet to ensure you're meeting your daily needs.

## Quick Tip
Add a serving of yogurt or a handful of almonds to your snacks to boost your calcium intake throughout the day.
