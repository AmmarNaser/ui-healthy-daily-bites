# Limit Caffeine Intake for Better Sleep

## Short Health Tip
Reducing caffeine consumption, especially in the afternoon and evening, can significantly improve your sleep quality and duration.

## Simple Explanation
1. **Point 1**: **Caffeine is a stimulant**: It can interfere with your ability to fall asleep and stay asleep because it keeps your brain alert and awake.
2. **Point 2**: **Timing matters**: Consuming caffeine too late in the day can negatively affect your sleep, as its effects can last for several hours.
3. **Point 3**: **Personal sensitivity varies**: Everyone metabolizes caffeine differently; some people may feel its effects even after several hours.
4. **Point 4**: **Limit caffeine sources**: Coffee, tea, soda, and chocolate contain caffeine, so be mindful of when and how much you consume.
5. **Point 5**: **Switch to decaf**: If you enjoy coffee or tea, try switching to decaffeinated versions in the afternoon and evening to help your body wind down.

## Quick Tip
Consider setting a cutoff time for caffeine, such as 2 PM, to help improve your sleep quality tonight.
