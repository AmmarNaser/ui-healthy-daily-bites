# Drink Smart: Hydration During Workouts

## Short Health Tip
Staying properly hydrated during exercise is vital for optimal performance and recovery. Make it a habit to drink water before, during, and after your workouts to keep your body functioning at its best.

## Simple Explanation
1. **Fluid Loss**: When you exercise, you lose fluids through sweat and breathing, which can lead to dehydration if not replaced.
2. **Performance Impact**: Even mild dehydration can impair your physical and cognitive performance, making your workout feel harder than it is.
3. **Recovery**: Proper hydration helps your muscles recover after exercise by improving nutrient delivery and reducing soreness.
4. **Hydration Needs**: Your hydration needs can vary based on exercise intensity, climate, and duration, so listen to your body and adjust accordingly.
5. **Electrolytes**: In longer workouts, consider replenishing electrolytes lost in sweat, especially if you’re exercising for over an hour.

## Quick Tip
Keep a water bottle handy during your workouts and aim to sip water regularly instead of chugging it all at once.
